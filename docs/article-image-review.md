# Original article-image publication review

Reviewed: 2026-10-04 (UTC)

## Result and release boundary

The explicit-license review did not find a blanket grant for original article images. That result is **not** a conclusion that every small preview requires individual permission. A second review assessed the statutory route below, which is the basis of the approved, limited pilot: **11 reduced previews**, not full-resolution restoration or permission-confirmed images.

Japan Copyright Act Article 47-5 can cover minor use incidental to qualifying location-search services. The Agency for Cultural Affairs expressly includes curated directory-type and provider-selected/push searches in Q35; Q29–31 explain the subordinate, minor and non-prejudicial use requirements. [Official guidance](https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/chosakuken/hokaisei/h30_hokaisei/pdf/r1406693_17.pdf), [Act](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/345AC0000000048/), [Regulation](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/345M50000080026/).

The pilot uses actual low-resolution cover images only beside source links in discovery cards. The 160px cap is a conservative engineering limit, **not a statutory safe harbor**. Access restrictions and customary search opt-outs must be respected; preparation copies protected; business operators undertake advance compliance review and publish contact information. Source attribution is required where customary. A removal process does not cure a use that otherwise fails these conditions.

The selected 11 records were individually checked for successful page/image access, source exclusions and visual suitability. Generic default images, substantive teaching diagrams and a group photo with little preview value were omitted. Sources with explicit content restrictions (Upswing and PokerCoaching) are excluded from this pilot. GTO sources remain unverified for this pilot after direct requests returned 403; this is not a determination that their every blog use is forbidden.

The US cases Kelly v. Arriba Soft and Perfect 10 v. Amazon support certain search-thumbnail uses on their facts; they are not worldwide clearance. [Copyright Office Kelly summary](https://copyright.gov/fair-use/summaries/kelly-arriba-9thcir2003.pdf), [Perfect 10 summary](https://copyright.gov/fair-use/summaries/perfect10-amazon-9thcir2007.pdf). Public international commercial rollout still carries jurisdiction-dependent uncertainty; professional review is recommended before broader use.

The fixed publication manifest contains 1,244 links from 9 sources: 1,208 articles and 36 video links. The archived collection contains 1,406 records and remains unchanged. PokerNews and m Portal remain excluded. This review does not reopen collection, expand the source/title allowlist, publish excerpts, or contact rights holders.

## Explicit-license route (separate from the statutory pilot)

For a permission-based activation, a positive decision requires a source-specific license, publishing term, or rights-holder authorization that covers the actual image and intended use: display in a publicly accessible, ad-supported link directory, with the permitted delivery method and required attribution. Permission for a normal text link, an OG image URL, RSS availability, robots.txt allowance, or an absence of a scraping prohibition is not permission to republish an image. A provider's own stock-image license may not permit sublicensing to Potover.

An official embedded player is distinct from a detached thumbnail URL. Embeds must also preserve provider controls and satisfy their platform and advertising conditions. Merely finding an embed feature does not approve republishing all images returned by feeds.

## Checklist for future permission-based expansion

1. Record a verified permission/license URL or privately retained written authorization, review date, rights holder, exact covered images and article identities, commercial-display scope, delivery method, attribution and expiry/revocation terms. Never put confidential correspondence in this public repository.
2. Validate exact source and article identity against the unchanged title publication manifest. Do not use a source-wide switch where permission covers only particular assets.
3. Allow only reviewed HTTPS image origins and the approved image URL; reject credentials, unexpected ports, local/private hosts, data/script URLs, protocol-relative paths, query-bearing tracking URLs unless specifically justified, and unapproved redirects.
4. Keep image rendering optional with a stable icon/text fallback when absent or broken; preserve original links and source credits. Do not claim source endorsement.
5. Use the same allowlist/validator for the static JSON, browser data and public Worker API. Raw storage and authenticated internal export remain separate.
6. Update Japanese and English disclosures to match actual use, run all relevant tests and production export checks, review a preview, and verify publication counts and rendered output before release.

## Review limits

This was targeted read-only research of official policies, links and representative pages, not an exhaustive legal opinion or a new bulk collection run. No rights-holder outreach was sent. A future specific grant may change the decision; it must be reviewed rather than inferred from this report.
